# Day 65 - JavaScript Smart Solar Energy Management System

## Overview

Created a Smart Solar Energy Management System using HTML and JavaScript to monitor solar panel generation, track energy consumption, manage battery storage, identify low-charge batteries, and generate daily energy reports.

## Topics Covered

- JavaScript Classes
- Constructor and Objects
- Arrays
- Loops
- Conditional Statements
- Solar Panel Management
- Energy Generation and Consumption
- Battery Storage Management
- Low Battery Monitoring
- Energy Balance Calculation
- Solar Panel Efficiency Analysis
- Daily Energy Reports
- DOM Manipulation

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- JavaScript

## Practice

Performed the following operations:

- Displayed solar panel details
- Added new solar panels
- Calculated total energy generation
- Calculated total energy consumption
- Displayed battery storage details
- Updated battery charge levels with validation
- Identified batteries with low charge
- Calculated energy surplus or deficit
- Found the highest-generating solar panel
- Calculated average solar panel efficiency
- Generated a daily solar energy report
